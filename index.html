The original prompt

Create a cinematic 30–60 second animated short film about a samurai, built entirely using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

You are responsible for writing the story, designing the characters, directing the scenes, and implementing the animation.

Story requirements:

  • Tell an original, emotionally compelling story about a samurai.
  • Follow a recognizable hero's journey arc: introduction, challenge, struggle or transformation, and resolution.
  • Make the story understandable primarily through visual storytelling rather than dialogue.
  • Include multiple distinct scenes, meaningful character moment, and at least one visually impressive action sequence.
  • Deliver a satisfying ending within 30–60 seconds.

Art direction — Japanese ink painting:

  • The entire film should resemble traditional Japanese sumi-e ink wash painting brought to life.
  • Use expressive black brush strokes on textured, off-white Japanese paper.
  • Keep the palette predominantly black, white, and grey, with extremely limited accent colours if necessary.
  • Incorporate ink bleeding, brush textures, flowing fabric, wind, ink splashes, and atmospheric effects.
  • Emphasize strong silhouettes, dramatic compositions, negative space, and cinematic camera movement.
  • Make the visuals feel hand-painted, artistic, and intentional rather than generic digital animation.

Animation requirements:

  • Use HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Canvas, or SVG.
  • Cinematic 16:9 widescreen presentation.
  • Smooth character animations, environmental effects, camera movements, and scene transitions.
  • Include Play and Replay controls.
  • The film should automatically play from beginning to end in approximately 30–60 seconds.
  • This must feel like an animated film, NOT a slideshow of static images.
  • Do not use pre-rendered videos or external AI video-generation services. The animation must be rendered through code in the browser.
